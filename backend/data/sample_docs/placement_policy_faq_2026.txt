Placement Policy FAQ 2026 — TNPC, PlacePilot AI

Q: Who is eligible for campus placement?
A: A student is eligible if they have CGPA 6.0 or above and no more than 1 active backlog at registration (Placement Policy 2026 Sec 1.1). Branch cutoffs apply per drive.

Q: What is the registration deadline?
A: Registration with TNPC by 30 August 2026, with verified resume and faculty advisor approval (Sec 2.1-2.2). Late registrations not accepted.

Q: How many offers can a student hold?
A: At most 2 active offers. On receiving a 3rd offer, earlier offers are forfeited (Sec 3.1).

Q: What happens once placed?
A: Once placed (selected + offer accepted), student is withdrawn from all future drives (Sec 3.2).

Q: What about no-shows?
A: Two interview no-shows disqualifies remainder of season (Sec 3.3).

Q: Offer timeline?
A: Offers communicated within 48 hours of company release. Student has 5 working days to accept/decline (Sec 4.1-4.2).

Q: Stipend minimum?
A: Internship stipends below Rs 10,000 per month need written company justification (Sec 5.1). Median stipend published annually per sector (Sec 5.2).

Q: Code of conduct?
A: Sharing assessment material = disqualification (Sec 6.1). Reneging on accepted offer needs TNPC approval and is recorded (Sec 6.2).
